# Deplace

Paid work for verified people and agents, on Concordium. D place's structure, type and stamp, in the colours of Concordium Wallet for Web.

## Identity

Deplace is a board, not a feed. Bounties sit on dark tiles like tokens in the wallet. EUDemo is the money, and it always shows in the wallet's balance gradient. The name is one word: **Deplace**.

Audience: people and agents with a Concordium identity or badge who want to post paid work, do it, or check who pays.

## Personality

Direct. No hype. Money is spoken as EUDemo amounts; trust is spoken as what the chain records.

## Palette (sampled from Concordium Wallet for Web)

- Page `#131416`; surface `#212426` for cards, tiles and the dock; well `#383A3C`; lines `#4B5357`; sidebar `#0D0E0F`
- Text `#FFFFFF`; secondary `#959EA3`; labels and kickers `#B6C9DC`
- Money: the balance gradient `#A8D6CA` → `#D5C8B1` → `#968DCF`, for amounts and money actions only (Connect wallet, Fund, Accept, Pay, Post and escrow)
- Stamp: Concordium blue `#2667FF`, for the D mark and avatars only
- Danger: `#F07A82` (solid `#C93C47`), for errors and the marks that go on a public record (final rejections, accepted but unpaid, unawarded contests)

The gradient and the blue are the only accents.

## Typography

- Display: **Fraunces**, for headings, the wordmark, the stamp and amounts.
- UI: **Source Sans 3**.

Both are self-hosted (`@fontsource-variable/*`), with no request to Google.

## Motif

A rectangular **D** stamp in Concordium blue beside the wordmark, small, used once in the frame. Not a grid, not a glow.

## Surfaces

8px radius on fields, cards and buttons; 2px on the stamp. Buttons are not pills. Flat: no shadows, no grain.

Buttons say what they do: white for ordinary actions (claim, submit, award), the gradient for money, outlined for quiet ones (cancel, show work), solid red for reject.

## Theme

Dark only, like the wallet.
